# Feature: Perks

The **Perks** drawer (opened from the app bar) lets you configure in-game bonuses that affect how many crafts are needed and how much XP you earn.


---

## Bonuses

These toggles reduce the number of raw crafts the calculator schedules.

### Scroll of Cleansing

When active, the calculator expects a 10% chance to save a secondary ingredient per craft. Input quantities are reduced accordingly.

### Factory outfit

When active, the calculator accounts for the ⅛ chance of receiving 4 doses instead of 3 on standard 3-dose recipes. This reduces the number of crafts needed to hit a dose target.

### Modified botanist mask

Adds a +5% chance to receive a duplicate potion per craft. The calculator schedules fewer crafts to compensate.

### Portable well

Adds a +5% duplicate-potion chance, stacking with the botanist mask. Enabling the **Brooch of the Gods** toggle (only available when the well is on) upgrades the well's bonus to +10%.

---

## XP Boosts

These controls do not change craft counts — they only affect the XP figures shown on each crafting step.

### Perfect juju potion

Adds +5% XP for combination-potion steps only.

### Clan fealty

Slider from 0–6%. Set this to your current fealty rank (1% per rank, up to rank 6).

### Botanist's outfit XP

Slider from 0–6%. Set this to the number of outfit pieces worn (1% per piece, 6% for the full 5-piece set).

### Custom XP %

Free-text field for any other additive XP bonuses not covered above.

A **Total XP boost** summary shows the combined percentage. When perfect juju is on, the summary notes the higher rate for combination potions separately.
